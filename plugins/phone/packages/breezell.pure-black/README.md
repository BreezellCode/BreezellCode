# Pure black

A theme of true black for OLED screens: the grounds of the dark theme made black and its edges deeper, Breezell's green kept. Less light, less battery.

Built into the Breezell phone app: it is listed under **Plugins › Installed › On this phone**, where it is switched on or off, and its theme is chosen among the appearance cards (**Settings**).

It runs no code: the app draws the colours it names.
