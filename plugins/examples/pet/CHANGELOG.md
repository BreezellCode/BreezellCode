# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Only her head and body catch the mouse: the rest of her frame lets clicks through to the chat, and the wheel over her scrolls it.
- She no longer takes a subagent or teammate at work for something waiting on you (with a Breezell that tells them apart).
- Turned off she costs nothing, and she costs less while idle.
- In a narrow chat she fits, whole. At any window zoom she stays under the pointer.
- Two windows no longer undo each other's progress or where you put her.
- She waits for her settings before showing, so a hidden pet no longer flashes as a window opens.
- A click answers whatever the chat is doing. Made small she fades at the edges instead of being cut off. Her sleepy Zzz is no longer mirrored.

## 1.0.0

- An anime-style wind spirit on the chat box who follows what the AI is doing, without reading anything said.
- Twelve levels by the Beaufort scale.
- Pat, stroke, feed, play, drag it along the box or up over the chat, and it floats back down; it wanders and sleeps by itself.
- Painted art, with hair that moves in the wind and eyes that blink.
- Its settings in Box > Feature Options, its card on a right-click.
- In every language of Breezell.
