# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- It floats outside the window only while something is going on: the agent working, waiting for you, just done or failed, or a task running. With nothing to show it stays in the title bar, and once all is quiet it flies back there. It no longer hovers over other apps to say All quiet.
- Outside the window it is as tall as in the title bar (26 px; it was 32), so it no longer grows as it leaves.
- At rest the island outside says your workspace's name, as the title bar's does. All quiet is gone, from the panel's headline too.
- Minimizing Breezell on Windows no longer sends it in from the corner of the screen: where Breezell says a minimized window's title bar is off the screen, it comes down from above the top of the screen and goes back up there.
- The pages and the tests build on Windows too.

## 1.0.0

- A capsule in the middle of Breezell's title bar that shows what the agent is doing, waiting for you, done or failed, and the build or test task that runs.
- At rest it is the search bar: your workspace's name, and a click opens the search.
- Leave Breezell or minimize it and it flies out to the top of the screen, above every app, saying All quiet when nothing goes on; come back and it flies home.
- A click opens it into a panel: your conversations with what each is doing, its clock and the last words of its reply, the tasks, Back to editor, and a click on a conversation goes straight to it.
- One island for every Breezell window, with each window in the panel and Go to switch.
- Floats on Windows, macOS and Linux under X11; stays in the title bar on a Wayland session.
- Its setting in Box > Feature Options.
- In every language of Breezell.
